How it works

1. Answer fourteen questions

Name him, list him at a position, pick a height and a build, then answer questions about who he is. The answers shape his stats, his habits and how much taller he gets. You never type a number, and you do not see one until you sign him: scouts get a range and an opinion, same as you.

2. Get a roster spot

New players sit as pending until the commissioner drops them onto a Prep roster. After that they show up in the league site like anybody else.

3. Spend every week

One point per simulated week. Cheap early, brutal at the top: 1 a point under 50, 2 to 69, 3 to 84, then 5. Potentials cost double.

4. Declare when you want

Prep to College, College to the pros, on your schedule. Declare early and take your lumps, or stay and dominate.
